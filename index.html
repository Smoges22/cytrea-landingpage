FAQ

Important answers before launch.

Is Cytrea an agency?

No. Cytrea is a marketplace and workflow tool that helps providers and caregivers connect directly.

Is Cytrea free for caregivers?

Yes. Caregiver access is free during launch.

Who can use Cytrea?

Caregivers, Adult Family Home providers, and approved vendor partners.

Does Cytrea do background checks?

No. Providers are responsible for their own hiring requirements, background checks, credential verification, and compliance.

When will the app be available?

App Store and Google Play links will be added once approved and live.

Will v2 replace cytrea.com now?

No. The current production site stays live until Sam approves a preview switch.
