A few useful answers

Before you get started.

Help & resources→
Who is Cytrea for?

Adult Family Home providers and caregivers, with an initial focus on Washington AFHs.

Is Cytrea free for caregivers?

Yes. Completely free for caregivers. Build a profile, organize credentials, find AFH jobs, apply, and communicate with providers.

Can I download Cytrea today?

iPhone users can download Cytrea on the App Store. Android is in closed early access for approved testers.

Does Cytrea verify credentials or hire caregivers?

No. Cytrea is a marketplace, not an employer, staffing agency, or verification service. Providers are responsible for background checks, credential verification, and hiring decisions.
