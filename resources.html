A few common questions.

How do I join Android Early Access?

Use Android Early Access to join the Google Play test. Contact support if you need help with testing access.

Does Cytrea check credentials?

Cytrea lets caregivers share credentials. Providers are responsible for verifying them and completing their own hiring checks.

How do I contact support?

Visit Support or email support@cytrea.com.

Is Cytrea free for caregivers?

Yes. Completely free for caregivers.
