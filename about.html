About Cytrea

Built close to the work.

Washington AFH roots. A practical belief that caregiver hiring can be more direct, organized, and accessible.

Real hiring challenges.
A more focused response.

Cytrea was created by Adult Family Home operators in Washington who understand the effort behind each new hire: sharing an opening, reviewing a caregiver’s background, and keeping conversations moving.

We built a marketplace that brings those steps closer together. Providers can organize hiring activity. Caregivers can show their experience and pursue opportunities directly.

Our mission is simple: make caregiver hiring more direct, organized, and accessible, with caregivers and providers at the center.
