About Cytrea

Built by Adult Family Home operators for real caregiver hiring challenges.

Cytrea is focused on improving direct connection and workflow clarity between Adult Family Home providers and caregivers.

Why it exists

Caregiver hiring needs a clearer path.

Adult Family Home hiring often happens across scattered channels, repeated questions, and inconsistent follow-up. Cytrea brings the core workflow into one focused experience while keeping hiring responsibility with providers.

Operator-informed

Designed from practical Adult Family Home staffing challenges.

Direct connection

Built for provider-to-caregiver communication, not agency positioning.

Caregiver visibility

Caregivers can present experience, availability, and credential details clearly.

Launch discipline

Pricing, store links, and production switching remain approval-gated.

Cytrea helps Adult Family Home providers and caregivers connect through a direct, focused hiring workflow.
