About Cytrea

Built close to the work.

Washington AFH roots. A practical belief that caregiver hiring can be more direct, organized, and accessible.

Why we built Cytrea

Real hiring challenges.
A more focused response.

Cytrea was created by Adult Family Home operators in Washington.

It brings the steps behind each new hire closer together: sharing an opening, reviewing a caregiver’s background, and keeping conversations moving.

Providers can organize hiring activity. Caregivers can show their experience and pursue opportunities directly.

Our mission

Caregivers and providers at the center.

Make caregiver hiring more direct, organized, and accessible.

What guides us

A focused way forward.

01

Specific, not generic.

We focus on Adult Family Homes, starting with the Washington AFH community.

02

People, not placements.

Cytrea connects caregivers and providers. We are not an employer or staffing agency.

03

Clarity, not promises.

We organize shared information. Providers remain responsible for verification and hiring.
